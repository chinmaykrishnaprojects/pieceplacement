Why trust this read — the interpretability metric

Interpretability is defined here as a measured quantity, not a label:
I(system) = R² of a linear model predicting the system's judgement from human-nameable features.

I = 1 means the judgement is a human concept (a material count). I near 0 means it is opaque. The tools driving this report sit high on that scale, which is what lets the report explain why in words a coach would use — rather than just handing back a number.
